How scoring works

  • Truth: hourly PM2.5 from OpenAQ (data from CPCB / IITM monitors), fetched every hour.
  • Only hours with at least 3 readings count, and each file's RMSE is pooled over all four stations. A team ranks by its better file, as on Zindi.
  • This is not Zindi's official score. Zindi uses its own copy of the data and may count hours differently. Official results come from Zindi.
  • Team names must match a name on the Zindi leaderboard (any capitalisation). Please upload only your own team's files.
